# GEAR: Global Energy Adaptive Resilience - Architecture

## The Fundamental Principle
**GEAR is a Global Energy Decision Intelligence Platform** that builds a living digital twin of the interconnected energy system, detects emerging disruptions, predicts their probability, simulates cascading consequences, optimizes resilient responses, and provides explainable strategies for human decision-makers.

### Observe → Understand → Predict → Simulate → Optimize → Explain → Decide → Act → Learn

## Two-Brain Intelligence
GEAR operates using two distinct intelligence layers:
1. **Operational Brain**: "What should we do today?" (Procurement, Routing, Logistics, Emergency Response)
2. **Strategic Brain**: "What should we build over the next 5–20 years?" (Infrastructure, Renewables, Nuclear, Partnerships)

## Global Data Fabric to Decision Pipeline
1. **Real World** feeds **Global Data Fabric** (Geopolitics, Energy, Logistics, Economy, Weather).
2. **Event Intelligence** performs cross-source fusion to detect weak signals.
3. **Temporal Knowledge Graph** maintains entity dependencies.
4. **Global Digital Twin** manages Current, Forecast, and Counterfactual states.
5. **Scenario Engine & Cascade Simulator** evaluate multi-commodity impacts.
6. **Optimization Engine** generates Adaptive Procurement and Strategic Reserve plans.
7. **Explainable AI** provides human-readable evidence and confidence scores.
8. **Five Operating Modes** surface this to human decision-makers.

## Five Operating Modes
1. **Global War Room**: Global map, live risks, emerging threats.
2. **Scenario Lab**: "What if X occurs?" simulations and cascades.
3. **Response Orchestrator**: "What should we do now?" (Action plans).
4. **Strategy Lab**: "What should we build for the future?" (2030-2050 investment).
5. **Decision Center**: Human approval with evidence, confidence, and audit trails.
