# GEAR - Data Ingestion Architecture

## Global Data Fabric
GEAR combines heterogeneous global intelligence, not just news APIs:
- **Geopolitics**: Sanctions, Conflicts, Policies
- **Energy**: Oil, Gas, LNG, Coal, Power
- **Maritime**: Ships, Ports, Routes, Delays
- **Economy**: Prices, GDP, FX Rates, Inflation
- **Weather**: Cyclones, Heatwaves, Floods

## Event Intelligence Engine (Fusion)
This is where raw information becomes intelligence.
`News/Satellite/Sensors → Extraction → Entity Resolution → Classification → Cross-source Validation → Event Fusion → Emerging Threat`

Example: Military activity + Insurance premiums ↑ + Ships changing routes = Emerging Chokepoint Disruption.
This allows GEAR to detect weak signals before a major disruption becomes obvious.
